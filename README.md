

# Investment Valuation & LBO Analysis: Adidas AG

## Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive financial valuation and private equity feasibility study for Adidas AG. It integrates traditional fundamental analysis with data-driven modeling to assess intrinsic value and potential investment returns.
All historical financial data : Balance Sheet, Income Statement and Cash flow are taken from the Official Adidas Website Annual Fin Statement

## Key Investment Highlights
* **Intrinsic Value:** Identified a **40.3% valuation upside** with an implied share price of **189.28** (vs. market price of 134.90).
* **Operational Turnaround:** Modeled a recovery in **ROE from -1.21% (2023) to 24.48% (2025)**, driven by a **23% improvement in asset turnover** since 2021.
* **LBO Returns:** Projected a **Base Case IRR of 11.97%** and a **1.76x MOIC** through a simulated 5-year leveraged takeover.

## Technical Methodology
### Financial Modeling (Excel)
* **3-Statement Model:** Developed a dynamic forecast with a **3.36% Revenue CAGR** and an **11.65% target EBITDA margin** by 2030.
* **DCF Analysis:** Built a bottom-up **WACC of 6.71%** utilizing Damodaran default spreads and relevered beta calculations.
* **DuPont Decomposition:** Performed a 3-stage attribution analysis to isolate net margin expansion and asset efficiency as primary value drivers.
* **LBO Framework:** Structured debt paydown schedules and exit sensitivity matrices (9.0x - 11.0x EBITDA multiples).
